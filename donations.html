WHY THIS WORK MATTERS

Receipts over narratives.

209 Audit exists to document what’s happening in public institutions, ask hard questions, and give people the receipts to demand better. When accountability disappears, whole communities pay the price for years.

This work is built on a simple conviction: the people of Merced County and the Central Valley deserve to see what’s happening in the institutions that shape their lives.
