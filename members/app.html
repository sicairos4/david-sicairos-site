Intentional Morning

Start with one action small enough to repeat. This editable 11-minute example is a starting point, not a requirement. Choose your own start time, shorten steps, or turn them off.

Consistency before intensity. A micro-habit is the smallest version you choose to practice: one sip, one gentle stretch, one sentence, or opening one task. Repeat your small version before deciding whether to add more. A missed morning is not a debt to repay.
