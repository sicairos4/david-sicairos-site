LIFESTYLE ID CREDENTIALS GUIDE

Present the work behind your identity.

Organize the learning, qualifications, projects and contributions you choose to share. This page is a planning guide: it does not verify qualifications, issue badges or publish a public profile.

PLAN YOUR PROFILE

Start with five steps.

1. Define your direction
Write one sentence about who you are becoming.
2. List your strengths
Identify 3–5 strengths you can show through real work.
3. Record your learning
List completed courses or qualifications you can substantiate; a starter-guide visit is not a certificate.
4. Choose what to share
Keep private routines and reflections private unless you deliberately choose to include them.
5. Link your work
Add projects, roles, or contributions you have permission to show.

BADGE CONCEPTS

Possible evidence categories.

These examples describe possible categories, not badges awarded or verified by this page.

Realignment Badge

Evidence could include completed learning and a practical project you choose to share.

Wellness Badge

Evidence could include relevant completed learning without exposing private health or routine records.

Builder Badge

Evidence could include a completed project and the work supporting it.

Contributor Badge

Evidence could include permitted examples of writing, research, organizing or community work.
