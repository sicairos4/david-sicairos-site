WHAT IT IS

A living record of who you are becoming.

Lifestyle ID Credentials is not a static resume. It is a living profile that grows as you learn, build, contribute, and follow through on the things you say matter to you.

Identity

Your values, strengths, interests, and the direction you are moving toward.

Learning

Courses, micro-lessons, and skills you have completed through Sovereign University.

Habits

Consistent routines and practices that show how you show up day to day.

Work

Projects, roles, contributions, and real work you can point to.

Contributions

Ways you have supported your community, field, or ecosystem.

Verification

Badges and confirmations that show what you have completed or demonstrated.

BUILD YOUR PROFILE

Start with five steps.

1. Define your direction
Write one sentence about who you are becoming and what you want to build.
2. List your strengths
Identify 3–5 strengths you can show through real work or habits.
3. Add your learning
Connect courses and micro-lessons you complete through Sovereign University.
4. Show your habits
Add the routines you actually maintain, not the ones you wish you had.
5. Link your work
Add projects, roles, or contributions that give your profile proof.

CREDENTIAL TYPES

Badges you can earn.

Realignment Badge

Earned by completing realignment-focused courses and building a consistent practice.

Wellness Badge

Earned by completing wellness micro-courses and maintaining a simple routine.

Builder Badge

Earned by completing Systems Studio learning and shipping a real project or workflow.

Contributor Badge

Earned by supporting the ecosystem through writing, research, organizing, or community work.

PRIVACY & CONTROL

You decide what is public.

Your Lifestyle ID profile is built in layers. You choose what stays private, what is shared publicly, and what is verified through the ecosystem.

Private layer
Your personal notes, goals, and drafts stay with you.
Shared layer
You choose which strengths, habits, and learning to make visible.
Verified layer
Badges and confirmations show what you have completed or demonstrated.
