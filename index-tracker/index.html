The Master Tracker is the working index behind 209 Audit: every agency and named subject in one searchable list, grouped into school districts, cities and county government, authorities and special districts, courts and law enforcement, state agencies, and the developers, vendors, and private parties who appear in the records. Every card explains in plain words why that name is in the tracker, what is still outstanding, and what happens next.

Use: Search the index, tap a card to open details, and optionally check the small box to select cards. Print / Save PDF creates a complete report in the current page and opens the device print dialog.
