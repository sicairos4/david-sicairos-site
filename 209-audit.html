THE STORY

Why 209 Audit exists.

209 Audit began as a response to being pushed out—and turned that pressure into purpose. What started as a personal fight for clarity became a doctoral-level study of the same leadership systems that created the problem, now examined through cybernetics and organizational leadership.

The creation of 209 Media was the answer: a platform built to counter fragmented, old-money-owned local narratives with something different—receipts, public records, and transparent documentation. Where other outlets may carry bias or split coverage, 209 Media runs on evidence.

HOW IT WORKS

From record to receipt.

209 Audit follows a simple, repeatable process: request, collect, organize, verify, and publish. Each step builds a clearer picture of what happened and who was responsible.

1. Request

Public records requests are filed through the CPRA process to obtain the underlying documents.

2. Collect

Responses, emails, reports, and supporting files are gathered into one organized system.

3. Organize

Documents are sorted into a searchable structure: timelines, topics, and source files.

4. Verify

Claims are checked against the actual documents before anything is published.

5. Publish

Clear summaries and source links are released so the community can see the receipts themselves.
