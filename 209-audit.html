THE STORY

Why 209 Audit exists.

209 Audit began as a response to being pushed out—and turned that pressure into purpose.

The creation of 209 was the answer: a platform built to counter fragmented, old-money-owned local narratives with something different—receipts, public records, and transparent documentation.

HOW IT WORKS

From record to receipt.

209 Audit follows a simple, repeatable process: request, collect, organize, verify, and publish.

1. Request

Public records requests are filed through the CPRA process.

2. Collect

Responses, emails, reports, and supporting files are gathered.

3. Organize

Documents are sorted into a searchable structure.

4. Verify

Claims are checked against the actual documents.

5. Publish

Clear summaries and source links are released.
