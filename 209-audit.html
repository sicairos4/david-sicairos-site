Three-step audit playbook

  1. Ask a focused question

    Identify the agency, specific existing records, custodians and dates. Keep proof of receipt.

  2. Preserve and compare

    Keep original records and source dates. Separate what arrived from what remains unanswered.

  3. Explain with receipts

    Locate the source, distinguish interpretation, consider counterevidence, state uncertainty and review privacy before sharing.
