THE PURPOSE

Make the record easier to follow.

209 Audit is built around a straightforward aim: organize public documents, explain what they cover and keep unanswered questions visible without presenting them as settled conclusions.

The collection is being built. Approved document entries will become available as they are added. Research notes, agency context and published explanations serve different purposes; none replaces reading the underlying source.
