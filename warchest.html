WHY THIS EXISTS

The work did not end at the firing.

“They thought firing me would end the work. It launched it.” That is the statement behind The Warchest: keep asking questions, keep organizing the record and keep turning difficult experience into something useful.

209 Audit centers the receipts. 209 Media and 209 Signal help explain them. The educational side turns lessons into tools and pathways people can use in their own lives and work.

The Warchest is the support fund beneath that connected effort. It helps sustain the time, documentation, research, communications and operating tools the work depends on. It is not a separate promise of a legal result or a particular story.
