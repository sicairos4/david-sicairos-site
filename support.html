HELP AND SUPPORT

Find the right next step.

Email sicairos4@gmail.com with the page link, what you were trying to do, the approximate time and a short description. Do not send passwords, access tokens, recovery links, payment-card numbers or private journal text.

Account access

Use the shared sign-in entrance or password recovery. A guest planner is separate from saved account records. Ask for help without sending sign-in credentials.

Free worksheets and printable copies

Starter worksheets can be printed or downloaded as text. Planning resources provide the site's planning-page entrance. A PDF/text copy is not a restorable account backup. Keep private exports secure.

Workstation backup and recovery controls are in Settings. For a download problem, include the resource name and device/browser, not the private contents.

Hosted-store orders and purchased resources

For dBeastco purchases, use the existing hosted store and the order/receipt information it supplies, if available. This website's support page does not confirm payment, resend a receipt or grant purchased access.

For missing delivery or a refund request, contact support with the product name, purchase date and order reference if available. Do not send full payment details. Eligibility and timing depend on the applicable purchase terms; this contact route does not create a refund guarantee.

Newsletter access or stopping emails

Use the newsletter signup entrance. Subscription is submitted on the hosted provider's form. To stop or change emails, use the unsubscribe or preferences link in an issue you received. If you cannot access it, email support from the subscribed address. Do not forward private confirmation tokens.

Research or media correction

Send the public page link, document ID or video title and a short explanation of the issue. Keep confidential source material out of a routine support message.
