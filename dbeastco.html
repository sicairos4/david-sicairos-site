HELPFUL STARTING TIPS

Build wellness without overcomplicating it.

Choose one foundation.
Start with sleep, water, movement, or a consistent meal routine.
Train with intent.
Match your training to your current schedule and goal.
Make recovery part of the plan.
Rest, hydration, and recovery are part of progress.
Keep the system simple.
A routine you can repeat is more useful than a perfect plan you cannot maintain.
