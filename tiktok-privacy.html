209 Media Publishing — TikTok App Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026. Operated by David Sicairos, 209 Media, Atwater, California. This policy describes only the 209 Media Publishing TikTok app. It does not describe the 209. Connect Workspace (see its own Privacy Policy) or any other site or service.

What the app is

209 Media Publishing is a single-creator publishing tool. It connects one TikTok account — David Sicairos's own account, @sicairosdavid — so he can publish his own short-form videos (civic news, education, and public-records reporting from Merced County, California) to TikTok. The only user of this app is David Sicairos. It does not serve the public, does not accept other users, and does not access any other person's TikTok account.

Information the app accesses

With David's consent through TikTok Login, the app accesses only the following, under the TikTok scopes it requests:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar. Used only to confirm the connected account is his own.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): the video files he chooses to post, and the title and caption text he writes for them. These are sent to TikTok to publish on his own account.

The app does not collect information from any other person. It does not access followers, friends, messages, comments, location, or analytics about anyone else. It does not collect anything from visitors to this website.

How information is used

Profile information is used only to verify the connected TikTok account. Video files and captions are used only to publish the videos he selects to his own TikTok account. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than publishing his videos.

Storage

TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored server-side in David's own secure credential store and used only to call TikTok's API on his behalf. Tokens are never shared, sold, or sent to any third party. A video file is processed for upload and is not retained by the app after publishing, except as part of his own content archive.

Sharing

The only recipient of data is TikTok itself — the platform the videos are published to. Data is not sold. Nothing is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

Revocation and deletion

Disconnect the app at any time in TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, and remove 209 Media Publishing. Removing it stops all access immediately. You can also email sicairos4@gmail.com and the stored tokens will be revoked. Revoking access does not delete videos already published to TikTok; those are managed in TikTok itself.

Contact and updates

Privacy contact: David Sicairos, sicairos4@gmail.com. Material changes will be published here with a revised effective date.
