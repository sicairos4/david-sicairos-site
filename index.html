209 AUDIT · MERCED COUNTY, CALIFORNIA

Public records, published where everyone can read them.

209 Audit is a citizen public-records project in California’s Central Valley. I file California Public Records Act requests with city, county, and school agencies, publish the documents they release, and explain what the records show, so any resident can check the record for themselves.
