SYSTEMS THINKING HUB

See the pattern. Build the next step.

Systems thinking helps you understand how things connect, identify what is blocked, and build the smallest useful next action.

1. OBSERVE — What is actually happening?\n2. MAP — What are the inputs, steps, blockers, and outcomes?\n3. LISTEN — What feedback is being ignored?\n4. IDENTIFY — Where is the breakdown occurring?\n5. ADJUST — What is the smallest useful change?\n6. REPEAT — Keep, revise, or remove it.
