LEADERSHIP & SYSTEMS

I study institutions as living systems.

My kinesiology background taught me to see the body as an interconnected organism. My doctoral work in Organizational Leadership, with a cybernetic-science concentration, applies that same systems lens to public institutions: how information moves, how feedback is handled, how resources are allocated, and whether accountability structures function.

Diagnose
Identify communication blocks, delayed feedback, and structural gaps.
Document
Follow public records, timelines, decisions, and institutional responses.
Inform
Turn complex systems into clear, community-facing education.
Redesign
Build frameworks that support transparency, feedback, and accountable leadership.

Habits. Lead. Model. Systems thinking is not only for organizations—it is a practical tool for daily progression, especially when motivation or executive function is low. Build the smallest useful habit, lead yourself through the next action, and model the system you want to see.
