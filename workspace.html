209. Connect Workspace

A personal planning and productivity application for organizing your day, your work, and your next step. The member dashboard is called Workstation.

What the application does

  • Plan morning routines and daily schedules.
  • Organize tasks, goals and focus sessions.
  • Write reflections and review saved days.
  • Prepare creator drafts and access learning resources.

Sign in to associate your saved planning records with your account. A separate synthetic demo is available without using your personal planning records.

Why Google sign-in is offered

Google sign-in provides an alternative to email and password. Google account identity information, such as your account identifier, email address, and profile information supplied during sign-in, is used by the Supabase authentication service to authenticate and identify your workspace account.

The workspace's browser sign-in code requests no additional Google API scopes. Its planner does not provide Gmail access, Google Drive access, or Google Calendar synchronization. Review the permissions Google displays before granting access.

Your entered tasks, schedules, reflections and drafts are workspace content, separate from Google account identity information. See the privacy policy for storage, sharing, retention and deletion information.
