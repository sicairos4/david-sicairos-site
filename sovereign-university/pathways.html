01 / CLARITY

Personal Brand Starter

Define who you help and what you stand for.

  1. Write: “I help ___ do ___ so they can ___.”
  2. List three values that guide your work.
  3. Choose three words for your public voice.
  4. Name one audience and the problem you help solve.
Your output: one brand statement, three values and three voice words.

02 / ASSISTANCE

AI Assistant Setup

Give your chosen assistant useful context without sharing private records.

  1. Write five sentences covering your work, audience, voice, goal and constraints.
  2. Choose three repeatable tasks you want help with.
  3. Create one prompt for each task.
  4. Keep responsibility for facts, privacy and your own voice.
Your output: an AI brief and three reusable prompt drafts. Output is not proof of accuracy.

03 / CONSISTENCY

Content Calendar Starter

Make a small publishing plan you can sustain.

  1. Choose three content pillars.
  2. Pick three publishing slots for the coming week.
  3. Match one pillar to each slot and draft its idea.
  4. Prepare three backup ideas and decide what to adjust afterward.
Your output: three planned ideas and three backups. This guide does not schedule or publish posts.

04 / CONNECTION

Newsletter Launch Kit

Build a clear promise before worrying about volume.

  1. Finish: “Each issue helps you ___.”
  2. Choose a cadence you can sustain.
  3. Draft one issue: one idea, one story and one action.
  4. Plan your signup invitation, consent wording and unsubscribe route.
Your output: a promise, cadence, first-issue draft and signup-flow plan.

05 / USEFULNESS

Digital Product Starter

Make a small solution before building a large catalog.

  1. Choose one problem your audience actually asks about.
  2. Pick a checklist, template or mini-guide format.
  3. Outline five to seven sections, then create a usable first version.
  4. Write one useful feedback question for a potential user.
Your output: a usable first version and a feedback question, not just a product name.

06 / PRESENCE

Website in a Weekend

A starter scope, not a guaranteed completion time.

  1. Plan Home, About, Work or Products, and Contact.
  2. Write one clear headline and one main action per page.
  3. Identify your page content, form destination and mobile layout needs.
  4. Publish when the core flow is ready, then improve one page at a time.
Your output: a four-page site plan and a clear next build action.
