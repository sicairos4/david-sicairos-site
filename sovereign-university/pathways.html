Launch Pathways

Six practical pathways to go from idea to a real, working presence. Each one ends with a concrete deliverable you can use immediately.

1. Personal Brand Starter

Define who you are, who you help, and how you want to be understood.

  1. Write one sentence: “I help ___ do ___ so they can ___.”
  2. List three values that guide your work.
  3. Choose three words for your voice (e.g., direct, warm, practical).
  4. Pick one audience and one problem you solve for them.
Deliverable: A one-line brand statement plus three values and three voice words.

2. AI Assistant Setup

Create a personal AI brief and a small set of reusable prompts.

  1. Write a 5-sentence brief: who you are, what you do, your audience, your voice, your current goal.
  2. List 5 tasks you want AI to help with (drafting, planning, research, editing, ideas).
  3. Save 3 reusable prompts, one per task.
  4. Set one rule: always review and personalize AI output before publishing.
Deliverable: A personal AI brief plus 3 saved prompts.
Build it in Lifestyle ID →

3. Content Calendar Starter

Plan a simple weekly rhythm so you always know what to post.

  1. Choose 3 content pillars (e.g., educate, document, connect).
  2. Pick a posting rhythm you can sustain (e.g., 3 posts per week).
  3. Assign one pillar to each posting day.
  4. Write 6 post ideas now, two per pillar.
Deliverable: A one-week content calendar with 3–6 planned posts.
Find ideas in the Media Library →

4. Newsletter Launch Kit

Launch a simple newsletter with a clear purpose and a sustainable cadence.

  1. Choose one promise: “Each issue helps you ___.”
  2. Pick a cadence you can sustain (weekly or biweekly).
  3. Draft issue #1: one idea, one story, one action step.
  4. Set up your signup link and share it everywhere.
Deliverable: A newsletter promise, cadence, and a drafted first issue.
See 209 Connect as a model →

5. Digital Product Starter

Turn one useful idea into a simple digital product or download.

  1. Pick one problem your audience asks about most.
  2. Choose a format: checklist, template, mini-guide, or short course.
  3. Outline it in 5–7 sections.
  4. Create version 1 and share it for feedback.
Deliverable: A completed v1 digital product outline (and ideally the product itself).
Start from the Business Idea Generator →

6. Website in a Weekend

Build a simple personal or business website with clear pages and calls to action.

  1. Choose 4 pages: Home, About, Work/Products, Contact.
  2. Write one clear headline per page.
  3. Add one call to action per page (subscribe, contact, buy, follow).
  4. Publish, then improve one page per week.
Deliverable: A live 4-page website with one clear CTA per page.
