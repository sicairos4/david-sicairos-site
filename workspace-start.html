A small start. A clearer day.

Your Workstation brings Morning, tasks, scheduled time and private writing together. You do not need to fill in every section.

Open my workspaceTry as a guest

Start with three things

  1. Open Morning. Choose a small routine, or skip for now.
  2. Add one task. Give it a time only if that helps.
  3. Choose Save day to keep account changes.

One workspace, a few clear places

  • Morning: routine steps, a priority and a manageable start.
  • Tasks: actions to finish. Scheduling and completion stay separate.
  • Schedule: time blocks, openings and protected sleep.
  • Journal: private writing, optional prompts and notes.
  • Focus: a timer linked to a task. Finishing a timer does not finish the task.
  • Settings: account access, preferences, exports and backup tools.
What saves, and what does not?

Opening or closing a panel does not complete steps, create blocks or save a day. Checking a step changes its check-in; Add to schedule creates unsaved blocks. Save day persists the account day when the backend confirms it.

Keep the page open until saving finishes. If saving is uncertain, export your edits before reloading. Guest plans are not automatically saved to your account.

Print or keep a copy on your phone

In Journal, open Print or save this journal. Choose Print / Save PDF or Save text to device. Journal copies include current writing, prompts and the lesson, including unsaved text; they exclude notebook entries. Store these private files securely.

The daily-plan output includes tasks and schedule, not journal content. Printing or downloading does not save the account day.

App window, browser and sign-in

The updated Android source embeds the online workspace without Chrome's toolbar. Your installed app changes only after installing a corresponding build. External links may deliberately open a browser.

The embedded preview supports guest and email/password access. Native Google return-to-app is still unfinished; browser sign-in does not transfer its session into this embedded preview. Production signing, installation, printing and account behavior require device acceptance.

Privacy and backups

Private writing does not publish automatically. Export files are unencrypted. Workspace backups cover supported saved data, not passwords or every browser draft. Read the scope shown by each backup/import tool.
