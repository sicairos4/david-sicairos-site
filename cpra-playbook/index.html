Start here

This is an educational organizational tool, not legal advice. It helps you ask clear questions, keep a timeline, and explain public records responsibly.

Timing: California agencies generally have 10 days to determine whether a request seeks disclosable records and provide an estimated production date.

CPRA Basics

Ask for existing writings about public business: emails, reports, contracts, calendars, spreadsheets, attachments, texts, recordings, policies, and data exports.

Focused requests work better

Use names, titles, date ranges, record types, systems, projects, vendors, and keywords.

Systems & Devices

Ask for the agency’s Enterprise System Catalog when you need to identify likely record locations.

“Please provide the agency’s current Enterprise System Catalog and identify the system(s), if any, used to store or manage records relevant to this request.”

Personal devices

Responsive public-business writings may be held on personal accounts or devices. Ask for a reasonable search where applicable.

FAQ

Must records arrive in 10 days?

Generally, the agency must make a determination within 10 days and provide an estimated production date. Production can take longer.

What if the request is too broad?

Narrow by dates, custodians, systems, record categories, and keywords.
