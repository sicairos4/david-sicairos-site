About David Sicairos

David Sicairos is an educator, independent publisher and creator of the 209 ecosystem. David holds a Master of Arts in Educational Counseling from University of Massachusetts Global and Bachelor of Arts degrees in Kinesiology and Psychology from California State University, Stanislaus. David's work connects exercise science, motivation, learning and systems thinking through practical educational resources.

Teaching focus: movement and exercise science, motivation and behavior, educational support and everyday systems. These areas inform the resources without being presented as additional degree titles or institutional endorsements.
