How records are organized

Organization → person and position → filing and amendments → disclosed interests → original document and page reference. Reporting periods and filing dates are stored separately.

Records enter a review queue before publication. Originals and amendments retain separate provenance. Publication excludes unnecessary personal contact details and preserves official redactions.
