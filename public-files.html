PUBLIC FILES DATABASE

Coming soon.

The searchable Public Files Database is being prepared. It will hold organized records, timelines, requests, and responses in one place.

WHAT IT WILL HOLD

What the database will include.

Records

Public records and supporting documents, organized for search and review.

Timelines

Chronologies that show how events, decisions, and responses connect.

Requests

CPRA requests and the responses received, tracked in one place.

Summaries

Clear summaries that explain what the documents show and why they matter.
