Sources, amendments and coverage

Agency → filer and position → reporting period → filing and amendments → document and source page.

Filing dates and reporting periods are separate. Originals and amendments remain separate records. Only published, redaction-reviewed documents appear. Source-file checksums identify the original; a reviewed public copy can differ.

A disclosed interest is not a finding of wrongdoing. The initial Form 700 collection excludes school districts, independent special districts and other cities. Other public-document entries require their own controlled review.
