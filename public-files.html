How this collection is organized

Agency → filer and position → reporting period → filing and amendments → document and source page.

Filing dates and reporting periods are separate. Originals and amendments remain separate records. Only published, redaction-reviewed documents appear. Source-file checksums identify the original file; a reviewed public copy can differ.

A disclosed interest is not a finding of wrongdoing. School districts, independent special districts and other cities are outside the initial collection.

Prepare a PDF for review — local only

This form prepares a draft receipt on your device. It does not upload, store or publish the PDF. Keep the receipt and original PDF together for controlled intake and redaction review.
